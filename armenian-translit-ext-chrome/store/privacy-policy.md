# Armenian Translit: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 25, 2026_

Armenian Translit is a Chrome extension that converts Armenian words typed
with Latin letters into Armenian script while you type.

## What the extension does with your text

To convert a word, the extension looks at the text before the cursor in the
field you are typing in, finds the word you just typed and replaces it with its
Armenian version. This happens entirely inside your browser.

- Your text is **not stored**, **not logged** and **not sent** anywhere.
- The extension has **no server** and makes **no network requests**.
- It does not read anything on the page other than the field you are typing in.
- It never touches password, email, URL or number fields.

## Data we collect

None. The extension collects no personal data, browsing history, analytics or
usage statistics.

## Settings

Your settings (whether conversion is on, and the websites where you turned it
off) are saved with Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.sync`. If you use Chrome
sync, Chrome syncs these settings between your own devices through your Google
account. The developer can't access them.

## Permissions

- **storage:** to save your settings.
- **activeTab:** to read the current website's address when you open the popup,
  so you can turn conversion off for that site.
- **Access to websites:** to convert text in text fields on the sites where you
  type.
